# Canyon kit

These are modular pieces for building Brawl-Stars-style arenas on a **1 m grid**. Each piece in `Models/` is its own `.glb`. All of them share a single material, `canyon_palette`, with one 64×64 palette texture, so pieces batch together.

Do not edit the meshes by hand. They are generated by `tools/blender/kits/canyon_kit.py`; change that script and rebuild (see the end of this file). `preview.png` shows a sample layout built only from these pieces.

## Placement rules
- **Position:** place each piece at the centre of its cell (`x + 0.5, z + 0.5`). Its origin is at ground level.
- **Rotation:** rotate only in 90° steps.
- **Floor:** every walkable cell gets a floor piece: `sand_tile*` or `water_*`. Their tops sit at y = 0.
- **Water edges:** `water_edge` has its bank on the north side (−Z in the engine, +Y in Blender).
  - `water_corner_outer` has banks on the north and east sides.
  - `water_corner_inner` has a bank patch only in its north-east corner. Use it where a pond bends around a land corner.
  - Rotate these pieces to face other directions.
- **Fences:** put a `fence_post` on every fence cell. Between each pair of neighbouring posts, place a `fence_rail` on the first cell. The rail runs from that cell's centre to the next cell's centre along +X; rotate it for the other directions.

## Pieces

## Rebuilding
1. Open `canyon_kit.blend`, or any Blender session, for example through Blender MCP.
2. Run:
   ```python
   ns = {}; exec(open("tools/blender/kits/canyon_kit.py").read(), ns)
   ns["build_all"](); ns["build_sample"](); ns["export_all"](); ns["render_preview"]()
   ```
